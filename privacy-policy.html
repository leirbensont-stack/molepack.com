Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MolePack ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website molepack.com (the "Site").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Email address: When you subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Correspondence: When you email us with questions or feedback.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: We use privacy-focused analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand site traffic. This includes page views, browser type, device type, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Cookies: We use minimal cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you our newsletter (if you subscribe).
  • To respond to your inquiries.
  • To analyze website traffic and improve our content.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect limited data:

  • Cloudflare: Website hosting, CDN, and privacy-focused analytics. Cloudflare does not track individual users across websites.
  • Amazon Associates and other affiliate programs: When you click an affiliate link, the destination retailer may set cookies on your browser. These are governed by the retailer's own privacy policy.

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access your personal data.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • The right to object to processing of your data.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@molepack.com.

CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Our website is hosted on Cloudflare, which provides enterprise-grade security including DDoS protection, SSL encryption, and secure infrastructure.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@molepack.com.
