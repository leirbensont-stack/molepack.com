Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency about how we make money.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

MolePack is a participant in various affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn a commission when you purchase products through links on our website. These commissions come at no additional cost to you.

Which Programs Do We Participate In?

We are currently enrolled in (or plan to enroll in) the following affiliate programs:

  • Amazon Associates: Amazon's affiliate program. Commissions range from 1-4% depending on the product category.
  • Blade HQ Affiliate Program: 5-10% commission on knives and EDC gear.
  • Optics Planet Affiliate Program: Up to 20% commission on optics and tactical gear.
  • Direct brand partnerships: Some brands offer direct affiliate partnerships with varying commission rates.

How This Affects Our Content

It doesn't. Here's our promise to you:

  • We never accept payment for reviews. Our reviews are based on our testing, not on commission rates.
  • We recommend the best product, not the highest-paying one. If a product with a lower commission rate is better, we recommend that one.
  • We disclose affiliate links. Links that may earn us a commission are tagged with rel="sponsored" in our HTML.
  • We purchase our own review units. We do not accept free products in exchange for coverage.

FTC Compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising, we disclose that we have a financial relationship with some of the companies mentioned on this website. We are independently owned and the opinions expressed here are our own.

Your Cost Is Never Affected

When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, the price you pay is exactly the same as if you had visited the retailer directly. The commission is paid by the retailer, not by you.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or how we make money, please contact us at hello@molepack.com.
