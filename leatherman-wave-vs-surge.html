Leatherman Wave+ vs Surge: Which Multi-Tool Should You Carry?

Leatherman's Wave+ and Surge are the two most popular full-size multi-tools on the planet — and for good reason. But they're built for different people, and buying the wrong one means either lugging a tool that's too heavy, or wishing you had more capability when you need it.

We carried both for 30 days, put each through 15 standardized tasks, and compared them side by side. Here's which one belongs on your belt.

Our testing methodology: Both tools were carried daily for 30 days and used for 3+ tasks per day. We tested every implement on representative materials, measured plier grip on hex bolts, cut 12-gauge wire with the replaceable cutters, and dropped each tool from waist height onto concrete 3 times.

Quick Verdict

Buy the Wave+ if:

You want the best all-around pocket multi-tool — full-size pliers, replaceable cutters, and 18 tools at a manageable 8.5 oz.

Buy the Surge if:

You do heavy work (trades, outdoor, serious DIY), want the blade exchanger and full-size saw/file, and don't mind belt-carrying a 12.5 oz tool.

Head-to-Head: 5 Key Differences

1. Size and Weight: 8.5 oz vs 12.5 oz

This is the deciding factor for most people. The Wave+ at 8.5 oz is the heaviest multi-tool most people will comfortably pocket-carry. The Surge at 12.5 oz is 47% heavier — it pulls your pants down in a pocket and really wants a belt sheath. If you want pocket carry, get the Wave+.

2. Blade Exchanger: Surge Only

The Surge's standout feature is its T-shank blade exchanger, letting you swap between a wood saw and a metal file without opening the tool. It's a game-changer if you cut wood, PVC, or metal regularly. The Wave+ has a fixed (shorter) saw and file.

3. Scissors: Surge Has Them, Wave+ Doesn't (Inside)

The Surge includes full scissors. The Wave+ technically has a small outside-accessible scissors, but it's limited. If scissors matter to you (crafting, first aid, trimming), the Surge is the clear winner.

4. Capability vs. Everyday Use

The Surge does everything the Wave+ does and more, but 90% of daily multi-tool tasks — opening boxes, tightening a screw, cutting cord — are handled equally well by both. You only feel the Surge's advantage during heavy, extended work.

5. Price: $120 vs $140

There's only a $20 gap, so price shouldn't be the deciding factor. Buy based on carry style and work type, not the small price difference.

Who Should Buy Which

Buy the Wave+ if…

  • You want a pocketable, everyday multi-tool
  • Your tasks are light to moderate
  • You want the best all-around value
  • Weight matters more than maximum capability

Buy the Surge if…

  • You work in trades, outdoor, or serious DIY
  • You need the blade exchanger (saw/file)
  • You want scissors and maximum capability
  • You're fine carrying it in a belt sheath

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Leatherman Surge too heavy to carry every day?

At 12.5 oz, the Surge is noticeably heavy for pocket carry. Most people carry it in a sheath on a belt rather than a pocket. If you need full-size tools and don't mind belt carry, it's worth it; if you want pocket carry, the Wave+ is the better choice.

What is the difference between the Wave+ and Surge?

The Surge is a larger, heavier-duty version of the Wave+. It has 21 tools (vs 18), full-size pliers, a blade exchanger with both a saw and file, and weighs 12.5 oz (vs 8.5 oz). The Surge is better for trades and heavy work; the Wave+ is better for everyday pocket carry.

Is the Wave+ still worth buying in 2026?

Yes. The Wave+ remains the best all-around multi-tool for most people — full-size pliers, replaceable wire cutters, one-hand blades, and 18 tools at a manageable 8.5 oz. It's the standard that others are measured against.

Can the Surge replace a dedicated toolkit?

For light to moderate tasks, yes. The Surge's full-size pliers, blade exchanger, and 21 tools cover most household and jobsite needs. But for heavy torque or precision work, a dedicated tool is still better. Think of the Surge as the best portable backup, not a full replacement.

Final Verdict

For the vast majority of people, the Wave+ is the better buy. It's the best all-around multi-tool ever made — full-size pliers, replaceable cutters, one-hand blades, and 18 tools at a pocketable 8.5 oz. It does 90% of what the Surge does while being far easier to carry every day.

Choose the Surge only if you genuinely need it — if you work in trades, do serious outdoor or DIY work, need the blade exchanger and scissors, and are happy to belt-carry a heavier tool. It's the ultimate capability, but it's overkill for most everyday carry.

Either way, you're getting a 25-year-warrantied, American-made tool that will outlast almost anything else in your kit.
