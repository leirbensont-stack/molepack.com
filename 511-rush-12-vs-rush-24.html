The 5.11 Rush series is the gold standard of tactical backpacks, and the two most popular sizes are the Rush 12 (24 liters) and the Rush 24 (37 liters). Same bombproof 1050D nylon, same 16+ compartment layout — but the size difference changes everything about how each one carries day to day.

We loaded both with gear and carried them for 30 days. Here's which size fits your life.

Our testing methodology: Both packs were loaded with a realistic daily load (laptop, water, jacket, tools) and carried daily for 30 days. We tested zipper durability with 500 cycles, compared carry comfort under load, and evaluated how each pack handles being under-filled versus fully loaded.

Quick Verdict

Buy the Rush 12 if:

You want a 24L everyday-carry pack that fits a laptop and daily essentials without bulk. It's the EDC sweet spot.

Buy the Rush 24 if:

You need 37L for travel, weekend trips, gym + work, or carrying bulkier gear — and don't mind the extra size.

Head-to-Head: 5 Key Differences

1. Capacity: 24L vs 37L

This is the whole decision. The Rush 12's 24 liters is the everyday sweet spot — enough for laptop, jacket, and essentials without bulk. The Rush 24's 37 liters is 54% more space, which is fantastic for travel but feels cavernous and under-filled for a normal commute. Buy based on your realistic daily load.

2. Size and Carry Comfort

The Rush 12 carries like a day pack — nimble, close to the body, easy to sling on and off. The Rush 24 is taller and deeper, which makes it more comfortable when fully loaded for travel, but it feels bulky and floppy when half-empty.

3. Laptop Fit: 15" vs 17"

If you carry a 17-inch laptop, the Rush 24 is your only option here. The Rush 12 tops out at 15 inches. This single factor decides it for many people.

4. Organization Depth

The Rush 24 has roughly 30 compartments versus the Rush 12's 16 — more dedicated pockets for cables, toiletries, and gear. Great for travel, but more pockets also means more places to lose small items if you don't need them.

5. Price: $99 vs $125

The $26 gap is modest. Buy based on capacity and use case, not price.

Who Should Buy Which

Buy the Rush 12 if…

  • You carry a laptop (up to 15") and daily essentials
  • You commute by foot or transit and want a nimble pack
  • You want the classic everyday tactical pack
  • Bulk is a concern

Buy the Rush 24 if…

  • You travel or do weekend trips regularly
  • You carry a 17" laptop
  • You need to haul gym gear + work gear together
  • You want one pack for everything

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Rush 12 big enough for everyday carry?

Yes. The Rush 12's 24-liter capacity fits a 15-inch laptop, a jacket, a water bottle, and daily essentials comfortably. It's the ideal size for everyday carry, commuting, and day trips.

Is the Rush 24 too big for everyday carry?

For pure everyday carry, the Rush 24's 37 liters can feel oversized and under-filled. It's better suited for travel, weekend trips, gym + work, or carrying bulkier gear. If you only carry a laptop and small items, the Rush 12 is the better fit.

What is the difference between the Rush 12 and Rush 24?

The main difference is capacity: the Rush 12 is 24 liters, the Rush 24 is 37 liters. The Rush 24 is larger, has more compartments, and can fit a 17-inch laptop, but it's heavier and bulkier. The Rush 12 is the everyday-carry sweet spot.

Can the Rush 24 be used as a carry-on?

Yes. The Rush 24's 37-liter capacity is within most airline carry-on limits, making it a solid one-bag travel option. Just be mindful that fully loaded with 1050D nylon, it can get heavy — pack light for flights.

Final Verdict

For most people, the Rush 12 is the right choice. Its 24-liter capacity is the everyday-carry sweet spot — big enough for laptop and essentials, small enough to stay nimble. It's the classic tactical pack for a reason.

Choose the Rush 24 if you genuinely need the space — for travel, weekend trips, a 17-inch laptop, or hauling gym and work gear together. It's the best one-bag option in the Rush lineup, but it's overkill for a simple daily commute.

Either way, you're getting 5.11's legendary 1050D durability and a pack that will last for years.
